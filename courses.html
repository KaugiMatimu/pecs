CIPS — Global Qualifications

CIPS qualifications set the benchmark for professional education in procurement and supply worldwide. CIPS supports its members’ professional growth and helps the wider business community raise standards in procurement. Its Diploma in Purchasing and Supply is a high-level qualification, accredited by the UK’s Office for Qualifications and Examinations Regulation (Ofqual) and listed on the Register of Regulated Qualifications.

Support ahead of upcoming CIPS exams

Support is available for each of the following qualification levels ahead of upcoming CIPS exams:

CIPS LEVEL 2

Certificate in Procurement and Supply Operations

The first of CIPS’ three entry-level qualifications. Suited to those starting in procurement and supply, those for whom it’s part of a broader role, or anyone with limited business background looking to move into the field. It’s also useful for managers in other disciplines, business leaders, or entrepreneurs who want to understand how procurement works and how it affects business performance. This level covers five core units.

CIPS LEVEL 3

Advanced Certificate in Procurement and Supply Operations

The second entry-level qualification, preparing learners for a procurement and supply career and for progression to the Level 4 Diploma. It comprises four core modules plus one elective.

CIPS LEVEL 4

Diploma in Procurement and Supply

The third entry-level qualification, made up of eight core modules built around the operational and managerial tiers of the CIPS Global Standard. Candidates begin with L4M1 (Scope and Influence of Procurement and Supply) and finish with L4M8 (Procurement and Supply in Practice); the remaining modules can be taken in any order.

CIPS LEVEL 5

Advanced Diploma in Procurement and Supply

Built around the managerial and professional tiers of the CIPS Global Standard, this level helps build confidence, strengthen the procurement function’s output, and support organizational goals. Candidates complete five core modules plus three electives.

CIPS LEVEL 6

Professional Diploma in Procurement and Supply

The route to MCIPS status, building on the knowledge and skills developed at Level 5. Candidates complete four core modules plus three electives.

PECS TRAINING APPROACH

Course design and delivery

PECS builds training around pre-course research and early input from key stakeholders, so the content responds to the audience and the work they do.

Course planning considers learner needs, organizational priorities, existing processes, available resources, and the subject being taught. Training objectives are set to address the needs identified during that planning.

Practical examples, questions, and individual or group exercises help learners connect course content to their work. Sessions can be delivered online or in person at a client-chosen location.

Variety

A mix of training methods keeps learning active and relevant.

Feedback

Oral and written checks reinforce what has been covered.

Small doses

Content is paced so learners can work through it step by step.

Participation

Group discussion gives learners an opportunity to contribute.

DELIVERY STRATEGY

Practical methods support active learning.

Training can combine a range of approaches to support discussion, participation, and workplace application.

Readings

Short learning materials introduce key ideas and prompt reflection.

Exercises

Case studies

Examples and discussion connect the subject to workplace experience.

Brainstorming

Open discussion helps teams explore options and address questions.

Presentations

Storytelling

Planning for your team
Course plans can be tailored to the location, duration, group size, materials, and learning methods that best fit the training need.
