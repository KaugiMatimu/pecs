Who We Are

OUR WORK

We help organizations in three ways: we train teams and individuals, source and place supply chain talent, and lead or support supply chain transformation projects, either running them ourselves or working alongside your team.

OUR HISTORY

Procura Edge Consultancy & Services (PECS)

Procura Edge Consultancy & Services (PECS) was established in 2020 and has grown into a leading East Africa and beyond provider of supply chain training, recruitment, and advisory services for organizations of every size.

Our people have worked across both large blue-chip firms and smaller enterprises, spanning sectors such as food retail, manufacturing, wholesale, construction, automotive, public administration, healthcare, education, and life sciences technology, among others.

At PECS, we shape solutions around each client's needs, supplying the skills, resource development, and strategic direction organizations need to perform well. Whatever the requirement—training for a team or an individual, recruiting a single specialist or a full department, or advisory support to design or deliver a supply chain strategy—we're set up to help whenever it's needed.

FORWARD THINKING

Building for tomorrow

We create sustainable solutions designed for long-term impact, helping our clients prepare for the future with confidence. Our vision is shaped around what lies ahead, while keeping strong foundations in the present so the value we build today continues to serve organizations well into the future.

OUR PROMISE

Committed to excellence

We are dedicated to delivering practical, cost-effective, and high-quality training and consultancy services with passion and purpose. We remain responsive to your changing needs, evolving our systems and practices to stay dynamic, relevant, and fully aligned with your growth.

OUR EXPERTS

Specialists who deliver practical impact

PECS brings together a strong team of specialists across supply chain management, marketing, economics, statistics, human resources, information technology, monitoring and evaluation, accounting, law, engineering, and project management. Our focus is on reducing risk, designing innovative solutions tailored to each client, and improving resource use, service delivery, and long-term value.

We assemble project teams to match the specific needs of each client, drawing on expertise in project management, survey design, data collection, and data analysis. Our staffing model reflects internationally recognised professional standards, allowing us to deliver a high level of professionalism and dependable service. We are committed to ensuring every client receives the quality of support they expect from the PECS team.

OUR APPROACH

Solutions built around your priorities

Every engagement is shaped around the unique demands of the client, their objectives, and the realities of the environment in which they operate. By combining technical expertise with practical insight, PECS develops solutions that strengthen performance, improve decision-making, and create lasting value.

CONSULTANCY

OPTIMISING YOUR SUPPLY CHAIN

The experience your customers have should mirror your strategy and value proposition, and it should do so at a cost your business can sustain. Your supply chain is the mechanism that turns that strategy into reality for the customer. When customers aren't receiving what they expect, when they expect it, at the price your business plan requires, that's where our consultancy work comes in.

OPTIMISING YOUR SUPPLY CHAIN

Building supply chains that perform strongly

A supply chain isn't just a process; it's the underlying engine that produces, delivers, and presents what you sell. At PECS, we focus on building supply chains that perform strongly and give clients a genuine competitive edge.

Our advisory work helps organizations procure and operate in ways that are strategic, ethical, efficient, and sustainable. Whether the issue is strategic, such as aligning the supply chain with overall business objectives, or more tactical, like managing supplier performance or cutting logistics costs, PECS is positioned to help.

We use a proprietary supply chain framework with clients to help them locate exactly where their pain points and opportunities sit. Alongside consultancy, PECS provides practical training for individuals and teams in procurement, supply chain, and project management.
